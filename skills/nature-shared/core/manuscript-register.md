# Formal manuscript register

Shared by `nature-writing` and `nature-polishing`. Apply when drafting or revising
formal manuscript text in either Chinese or English, including abstracts, main
text, captions, appendices and supplementary material. Follow the target journal
and the user's explicit choices; these rules are not specific to Nature journals.
They do not turn a requested lab notebook, test report, progress update, writing
plan or response letter into a manuscript.

## Write the scientific account

The manuscript must read as a scientific paper. Its organizing logic is the
research question, method, evidence, interpretation and scope, rather than the
chronology of what the author or assistant ran, checked, changed or discussed.
Use direct, precise academic language. Formality does not require inflated
vocabulary, impersonal passives or the removal of first-person statements.

- Lead results and discussion paragraphs with the finding or interpretation they
  establish, then supply the evidence and necessary qualification. Do not merely
  paraphrase successive panels or list completed checks.
- Describe methods in the order needed to understand and reproduce them.
  Sequential procedures, preprocessing and validation are legitimate scientific
  content when they affect reproducibility or interpretation.
- Explain an approximation through its purpose, definition and conditions of
  use. Do not frame it as reassurance to an imagined reviewer.
- State applicability through the tested conditions and evidence supporting the
  claim. Consolidate repeated caveats and speculative extension lists without
  suppressing substantive limits.

## Recognize and transform report-like narration

These are contextual diagnoses, not a list of forbidden words.

| Pattern | Manuscript treatment |
|---|---|
| Run history: “本轮重新计算了……”“目前已经核验通过”“我们又补做了一组测试” | State the resulting method or finding and its conditions. Preserve a scientifically relevant correction or failed test; omit operational status. |
| Repeated announcements: “下面我们将……”“本节首先介绍……下一节再验证……” | Use a transition that explains the scientific need or logical relation. Keep a concise roadmap where it helps the reader. |
| Commentary to the author or reviewer: “为避免审稿人误以为……”“这里需要提醒……”“后续可以补充……” | State the definition, distinction, evidence boundary or warranted implication directly. Keep writing suggestions outside the manuscript. |
| Administrative provenance: “本次整理的已发表势函数”“我们采用已有且公开的数据来做验证” | Name the scientific material/model/data and cite the source. Retain publication status, provenance or access conditions when they matter to the argument, attribution, ethics or reproducibility. |
| Validation ledger: one paragraph per check, long strings of percentage deviations or parameter pairs, “以上验证均通过” | State what the comparison establishes, with the quantitative evidence needed to assess it. Keep exceptions and the definition of the metric. Use or refer to existing detailed tables where appropriate; do not invent a table or silently discard data. |
| Repeated summaries or disclaimers across text, captions and conclusion | Give each passage its scientific function. Define once and reuse the term; keep captions independently readable and retain purposeful emphasis requested by the user. |

Do not mechanically ban “本文”, “本节”, “验证”, “已发表”, “we”, “validation”,
negative statements or cross-references. For example, publication status matters
in a comparison of preprints and peer-reviewed evidence, and a failed validation
can be central to a model's applicability. Judge the sentence's function.

## Preserve the evidence while changing the voice

Before cutting or merging a sentence, identify whether it carries a result,
definition, method, assumption, uncertainty, exception or scope condition.
Rewrite such information as scientific prose rather than deleting it.

Never improve the tone by removing model assumptions, screening rules or
exceptions, unsuccessful checks, untested ranges, distinctions between physical
quantities, or numerical procedures that affect the result. Do not turn
“consistent with” into a causal demonstration, or a tested range into a universal
claim. No new result, mechanism, citation or explanation may be invented to make
the narrative smoother. Operational debugging history may remain in a lab record; a
failed check that affects data validity or the scientific inference must remain
disclosed in the appropriate manuscript section.

There is no fixed quota for percentages, caveats, cross-references or repeated
terms. Keep what the inference requires. Do not move substantive material out of
the requested section without authorization. Synchronize substantive changes
across paired language versions when they are part of the task.

## Examples of the transformation

Use only the information actually supplied by the author. Bracketed text below
illustrates missing information; it is not evidence or submission-ready prose.

- “为避免读者误以为这就是实际芯宽，这里需要强调……” → “\(\zeta\) 表示固定参考剖面的半宽；弛豫后的芯宽由 PN 平衡剖面测量。” This states a distinction directly.
- “我们重新计算后发现长线偏差仍然较大，后续需要进一步验证。” → “在所测试的较长线条件下，PN 阈值偏离整线平均预测。” Retain any supplied magnitude or failed criterion, and identify an unresolved cause as unresolved rather than inventing one.
- “七组检查通过，另有一组没有通过，但目前保留。” → “七组数据满足输入筛选准则；第八组保留了未满足该准则的实现。” Keep the group identity, criterion and consequence if supplied; do not recast all eight as passing.
- “We reran the comparison and all tests passed, confirming excellent agreement.” → State the supported comparison, metric and range: “Across [tested conditions], [prediction] differed from [reference] by [reported metric].” Do not add “all tests passed” or “excellent” without a defined basis.

## Final register check

Scale this check to the request; a one-sentence edit does not require a full-paper
audit. Read the revised passage and its immediate context, and check wider
repetition when revising a section or manuscript:

1. Does each sentence serve the scientific argument or reproducibility, rather
   than report the work session or communicate with the author?
2. Do the paragraph openings expose findings and reasoning instead of repeated
   task announcements or figure inventories?
3. Have numerical detail, assumptions, exceptions and limits retained their
   scientific meaning and appropriate prominence?
4. Is repeated material serving a distinct purpose, and are necessary
   definitions, captions and language versions consistent?

Keep revision notes, plans, evidence-status maps, compilation results and
requests for missing information outside the manuscript text. For a document
edit, the document receives scientific prose; the conversation receives the
brief change summary. Missing evidence remains an explicit author-facing issue
or a clearly marked draft placeholder, never an invented fact or a concealed
omission.
